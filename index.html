Welcome to Ayurveda Life

Wellness is built from the small things you repeat.

Ayurveda is a traditional whole-person approach to living well. It considers how food, sleep, movement, seasons, stress, and relationships shape the way we feel.

This is not about following a perfect routine. It is about choosing a few kind, sustainable practices that help you feel more steady, nourished, and present.

Vata Dosha / Air + Space

Move with warmth and steadiness.

Vata is associated with movement in the body and mind: breath, circulation, nerve impulses, creativity, and elimination.

Core qualities

LightDryColdMobileQuick

In balance

Creative, flexible, lively, enthusiastic, and quick to learn.

When unsettled

Anxiety, dry skin, constipation, restless sleep, joint discomfort, or bloating.

Supportive choices

Warm cooked meals, calming routines, gentle oil massage, and a regular bedtime. Limit cold drinks, dry snacks, and excess fasting.

Pitta Dosha / Fire + Water

Make room for coolness and ease.

Pitta governs digestion, metabolism, body temperature, vision, intelligence, appetite, and hormonal balance.

Core qualities

HotSharpOilyLightLiquid

In balance

Strong digestion, sharp memory, courage, leadership, and good concentration.

When unsettled

Acidity, anger, skin rashes, excess sweating, ulcers, hair fall, or irritability.

Supportive choices

Coconut water, cucumber, watermelon, sweet fruits, mint, and coriander. Limit spicy or fried food, alcohol, coffee, and excess salt.

Kapha Dosha / Earth + Water

Invite lightness, variety, and momentum.

Kapha brings structure, lubrication, immunity, strength, growth, stability, and emotional calmness.

Core qualities

HeavyColdSlowStableSmooth

In balance

Calm, loving, patient, loyal, resilient, and full of steady stamina.

When unsettled

Sluggishness, weight gain, excess mucus, water retention, slow digestion, or low mood.

Supportive choices

Barley, millet, ginger, garlic, green vegetables, and lentils. Limit sugar, heavy dairy, ice cream, fried food, and excess sleep.

Panch Mahabhuta

The five great elements.

Ayurveda describes all of nature, including the human body, through five elements. Together they help explain physical structure, natural functions, and mental tendencies.

01

Akash Space

Openness and expansion. Reflected in body cavities and channels, and associated with awareness and creativity.

02

Vayu Air

Movement and lightness. Reflected in breath, circulation, mobility, imagination, and quick thinking.

03

Agni Fire

Heat and transformation. Reflected in digestion, metabolism, vision, courage, and discernment.

04

Jala Water

Fluidity and cohesion. Reflected in blood, saliva, lymph, compassion, and calmness.

05

Prithvi Earth

Stability and density. Reflected in bones, muscles, patience, and groundedness.

Dinacharya

A little ritual goes a long way.

Choose one practice and let it become familiar before adding another.

  1. 01Begin with a pauseTake three relaxed breaths before reaching for a screen.
  2. 02Nourish with attentionMake space for a warm, satisfying meal without rushing.
  3. 03Move in a way you enjoyWalk, stretch, practise yoga, or dance.
  4. 04Close the day softlyDim lights and create a gentle, consistent wind-down.
